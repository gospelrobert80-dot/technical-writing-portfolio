# How to Share a Google Drive Folder

Learn how to share folders in Google Drive using a computer or a mobile device.

## Step 1: Open the Share Menu
* **On a Computer:** Right-click the folder and select **Share**.
* **On a Mobile Device:** Tap the **three dots icon** next to the folder and select **Share**.

## Step 2: Choose Your Sharing Method

### Option A: Share via a Public Link
1. Under *General access*, click the access dropdown (defaults to *Restricted*) and select **Anyone with the link**.
2. Select a role dropdown to assign permissions (**Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**).
3. Click **Copy link** and share it manually.

### Option B: Share with Specific People via Email
1. Type the target email address into the **Add people and groups** field.
2. Select a permission level from the dropdown:
   * **Viewer:** Can view and download files.
   * **Commenter:** Can view, download, and leave comments.
   * **Editor:** Can add, edit, delete, and share files.
3. Check **Notify people** to send an automatic email alert.
4. Click **Send**.
